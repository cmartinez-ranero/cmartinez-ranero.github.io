Research

My research is in mathematical logic, mainly set theory, together with its interactions with general topology and number theory.

Set theory & topology

Combinatorics of the first uncountable cardinal: Aronszajn trees and lines, Suslin lines, and the structure these objects acquire under forcing axioms such as PFA and the Open Graph Axiom. I also work on cardinal characteristics of the continuum, MAD families, and set-theoretic topology.

Definability & number theory

Questions around Hilbert's Tenth Problem: undecidability of infinite algebraic extensions of $\mathbb{Q}$ and of $\mathbb{F}_p(t)$, and the Diophantine problem for addition and divisibility over rings of $S$-integers and related rings.

I completed my PhD at the University of Toronto in 2010 under the supervision of Stevo Todorcevic. From 2011 to 2013 I was a postdoctoral fellow at UNAM, campus Morelia, and from 2013 to 2017 an assistant professor at the Universidad de Concepción, where I am now an associate professor and Director of the Department of Mathematics.
